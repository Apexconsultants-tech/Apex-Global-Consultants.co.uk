Making Tax Digital for Income Tax Self Assessment (MTD for ITSA) changes how many sole traders and landlords report their income to HMRC.

## Who is affected

Individuals with qualifying income from self-employment and property above the threshold set by HMRC for each phase. Check the current thresholds on GOV.UK before relying on them.

## What changes

- Digital records kept in compatible software.
- Quarterly updates sent to HMRC.
- A final declaration after the tax year ends.

## What to do now

Choose compatible software, move your records across and run a test quarter before your start date.
