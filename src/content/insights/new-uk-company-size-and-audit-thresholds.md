For financial years starting on or after **6 April 2025**, the limits that decide whether a UK company is micro, small, medium or large went up by around 50%. Thousands of companies now fall into a smaller category, which can mean less reporting and, for many, no statutory audit.

## How the size rules work

- **Two of three:** a company is in a size category if it meets at least two of the three limits (turnover, balance sheet total and average employees).
- **Two years in a row:** you generally need to meet (or fail) the limits for two consecutive years to gain or lose a size.
- **Look back:** the new limits can be applied to the previous year too, which can bring the benefit forward.

## The new thresholds

| | Micro (+58%) | Small (+47%) | Medium (+50%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turnover | ~~£632k~~ → **£1m** | ~~£10.2m~~ → **£15m** | ~~£36m~~ → **£54m** |
| Balance sheet total | ~~£316k~~ → **£500k** | ~~£5.1m~~ → **£7.5m** | ~~£18m~~ → **£27m** |
| Average employees | 10 (no change) | 50 (no change) | 250 (no change) |
| Firms moving down a size* | 113,000 into micro | 14,000 into small | 6,000 into medium |

\*Government estimates of companies and LLPs moving into each category. Groups and LLPs use the same limits.

## What changes when you move down a size

### Large to medium

- No section 172(1) statement, and a lighter strategic report.
- You can use the medium-sized company accounts provisions.
- You can file simplified information at Companies House.

### Medium to small

- Audit exemption, although conditions apply (for example, if the company is part of a group).
- No strategic report.
- You can choose not to file the profit and loss account or directors' report, and can prepare abridged accounts with all members' consent.

### Small to micro

- A simplified micro-entity balance sheet.
- No directors' report or profit and loss account filed at Companies House.
- An audit may still be needed if your articles or shareholders require it.

## Directors' report: items removed

For large and medium companies, these disclosures are no longer required in the directors' report:

- financial instruments
- events since the year end
- likely future developments
- research and development
- branches outside the UK
- employment of disabled people (removed for small companies too)
- employee engagement
- customer and supplier engagement

## Coming later

Small and micro companies will have to file a profit and loss account, and abridged accounts will end. Companies House says this won't start before April 2028.

## What to do now

- Check your last two years' figures against the new limits, including the look-back rule.
- If you may now be audit exempt, check your articles, shareholder agreements and any bank or group requirements before you drop the audit.
- Review what you file at Companies House and what you can leave out.
