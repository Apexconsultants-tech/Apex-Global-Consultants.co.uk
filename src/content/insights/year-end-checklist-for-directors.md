A smooth year-end starts weeks before the balance sheet date.

## Before the year-end

- Reconcile every bank, card and loan account.
- Chase old debts and review any that will not be paid.
- Check that dividend paperwork matches what was paid.

## After the year-end

- Count stock and record work in progress.
- Gather details of new assets and finance agreements.
- Book a review meeting with your accountant.
