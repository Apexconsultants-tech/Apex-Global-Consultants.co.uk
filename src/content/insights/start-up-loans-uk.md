A Start Up Loan is an unsecured personal loan, backed by the government, for people starting or growing a business. You don't need collateral, but you do need to pass a credit check.

## At a glance

| Loan feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Amount | £500 to £25,000 per person |
| Interest | 7.5% fixed a year |
| Repayment term | 1 to 5 years |
| Support | 12 months of free mentoring |
| Fees | No early repayment fee and no application fee |

## Can I apply?

You must meet every one of these points:

- you are 18 or over
- you live in the UK and have the right to work here
- your business is based in the UK
- you are starting a business, or have been trading for less than five years
- you can pass a personal credit check

**It's a personal loan.** You are personally responsible for repaying it, even if the business stops trading.

**In a partnership?** Each partner can apply for their own loan, up to £100,000 per business.

## How to apply

1. **Check you qualify** against the list above.
2. **Apply online** through Start Up Loans, part of the British Business Bank.
3. **Work with an adviser.** A delivery partner helps you finish your business plan.
4. **Assessment.** Your credit is checked and your plan is reviewed.
5. **Get funded.** The money is paid to you, along with 12 months of mentoring.

### What you'll need

- a business plan
- a 12-month cash flow forecast
- a personal survival budget

## What it costs

Examples at the 7.5% fixed rate:

| Borrow | Over 3 years: monthly | Total interest | Over 5 years: monthly | Total interest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| £5,000 | £155.53 | £599 | £100.19 | £1,011 |
| £10,000 | £311.06 | £1,198 | £200.38 | £2,023 |
| £25,000 | £777.66 | £2,996 | £500.95 | £5,057 |

These use the standard repayment formula and are rounded, so your lender's figures may differ slightly. A longer term means lower monthly repayments but more interest overall.

Terms were checked on GOV.UK in November 2026. Always confirm the current terms with Start Up Loans before you apply.
