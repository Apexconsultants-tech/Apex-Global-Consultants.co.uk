The Financial Reporting Council's periodic review of FRS 102 brings the most significant changes to UK GAAP in a decade. For most companies they apply to accounting periods beginning on or after **1 January 2026**.

## What is changing

- **Leases.** Most operating leases come onto the balance sheet as a right-of-use asset and a lease liability, in a model based on IFRS 16, with some simplifications.
- **Revenue.** A new five-step model for recognising revenue, based on IFRS 15.
- **Other amendments.** Changes to areas including fair value measurement and disclosures for small entities.

## Who is affected

Companies preparing accounts under FRS 102, including many UK subsidiaries of international groups. Micro-entities using FRS 105 are not directly affected by these changes.

## What to do now

1. List your leases and gather the key terms.
2. Review your main revenue contracts against the five-step model.
3. Assess the impact on profit, net assets, covenants and tax.
4. Talk to your lender and auditor early if key ratios will move.
